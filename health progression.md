1. **Hospital Billing and Invoicing** – Manages patient bills for
consultations, treatments, procedures, tests and hospital services.
## 1. Hospital Billing and Invoicing
**Hospital Billing and Invoicing** is a module responsible for
calculating, preparing, managing, and issuing bills for all medical
services provided to patients by a hospital.
Its main purpose is to ensure that every service a patient receives is
properly recorded, charged, and included in the patient's bill.
### A. Main Functions
**1. Patient Billing**
- Creates a bill for each patient.
- Links bills to the patient's Registration ID.
- Records charges for consultations, treatments, procedures,
laboratory tests, radiology, medication, and other services.
- Calculates the total amount payable.
**2. Service Pricing**
- Maintains prices for hospital services.
- Sets consultation fees.
- Sets laboratory test prices.
- Sets radiology and imaging prices.
- Sets treatment and procedure charges.
- Sets medication prices.
**3. Invoice Generation**
- Generates official invoices for patients.
- Assigns a unique invoice number.
- Displays patient details and services received.
- Shows quantities, individual prices, total charges, discounts and
outstanding balances.
- Allows invoices to be printed or shared.
**4. Billing Status**
Tracks the status of each patient bill, such as:
- Unpaid
- Partially paid
- Fully paid
- Overdue - Cancelled

--- Page 2 ---
**5. Discounts and Exemptions**
- Records authorised discounts.
- Handles approved fee exemptions.
- Records reasons for discounts or exemptions.
- Identifies who authorised the adjustment.
**6. Billing History**
- Stores previous patient invoices.
- Shows services previously charged.
- Tracks outstanding bills.
- Allows authorised staff to review billing records.
### B. How It Works in the Hospital
Example:
A patient visits the hospital and receives the following services:
| Service | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Doctor consultation | TSh 10,000 |
| Laboratory tests | TSh 15,000 |
| Medication | TSh 8,000 |
| Treatment | TSh 7,000 |
| **Total** | **TSh 40,000** |
The module combines these charges into one invoice.
The invoice contains:
- Patient's name and Registration ID.
- Invoice number.
- Date of service.
- Description of each service.
- Individual service prices.
- Total amount payable.
- Amount already paid.
- Remaining balance.
### C. Users of This Module
- **Cashier:** Prepares invoices and manages billing records. - **Billing Officer:** Reviews and manages patient charges.

--- Page 3 ---
- **Hospital Administrator:** Oversees billing operations and
authorises permitted adjustments.
- **Management:** Reviews billing reports and revenue information.
### D. Difference Between Billing and Payments
These are two different responsibilities:
- **Hospital Billing and Invoicing:** Determines how much the patient
owes and generates the invoice.
- **Payments and Financial Management:** Records money received,
payment methods, deposits, refunds and financial transactions.
For example, Billing generates an invoice of TSh 40,000. The
Payments module records when the patient pays that amount.
**In simple terms:** Hospital Billing and Invoicing answers three
questions:
1. What medical services did the patient receive?
2. How much does each service cost?
3. How much does the patient owe the hospital?

### B. How It Works in the Hospital
Example:
A patient is discharged from a hospital ward.
The module:
1. Receives notification that the room is ready for cleaning.
2. Creates a cleaning task.
3. Assigns the task to a housekeeping worker.
4. Records the required cleaning procedure.
5. Tracks cleaning and disinfection.
6. Records completion.
7. Allows inspection where required.
8. Marks the room as ready for its next patient.
