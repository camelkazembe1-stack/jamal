How Paediatric Department works

What it does. Registers children with guardian details and links them to their Registration ID (and to the mother's record where appropriate), records consultations and paediatric diagnoses, monitors growth (weight, height, head circumference) and developmental milestones, manages common childhood diseases with age- and weight-appropriate prescribing, coordinates immunisation records, and manages paediatric admission and discharge.

Example. A two-year-old arrives with fever and vomiting: the child is identified, symptoms and measurements recorded, diagnosis documented, treatment prescribed, and follow-up scheduled.

Users. Paediatricians, Paediatric and Neonatal Nurses, Clinical Officers, Nutritionists, Laboratory Professionals, Paediatric Department Manager.
