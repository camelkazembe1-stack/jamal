Patient Admission

Record hospital admissions for existing patients — from the admission decision until the admission is completed, cancelled or handed over to discharge. Admissions link to the patient records and to the ward and bed records that already exist; nothing here registers a patient twice or rebuilds the ward system.

How admission works

Find the patient from the Patient List (never register them again), record the date, time, reason and the clinician’s diagnosis, choose Emergency or Planned, then save — as Pending when the process has started, or Admitted once confirmed. The admission appears in the Admission List immediately. When a bed is needed, the assignment reuses the existing Ward Management records; a transfer later moves the patient while the same admission stays open, and discharge closes it. Every previous admission of a patient stays in the history, never overwritten.

🚨 Emergency rule: treatment always comes first. No registration, admission paperwork or bed assignment may delay urgent care — save the emergency admission with what you know and let the records follow the patient.
