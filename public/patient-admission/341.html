New Admission

Admit an existing patient: find them from the Patient List, record the admission details, and save. The admission links to the registration that already exists — no second patient record is ever created — and the admission number is generated for you.

🚨 Emergency admission? Choose Emergency, enter the patient, the date, the time and the reason, then Save & Confirm — the diagnosis and the rest of the paperwork can follow the patient. Treatment is never delayed by this form.

1 · Find the patient

Search by Registration ID or by name — the patient must already exist in the Patient List (322).

2 · Admission details

Pending = the admission process has started but is not confirmed yet. Admitted = confirmed — the patient is recorded as an inpatient. A bed can be assigned afterwards from Ward Management; the success line below opens it with the patient preselected.
