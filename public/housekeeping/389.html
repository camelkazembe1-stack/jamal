How Housekeeping and Environmental Services works

What it does. Creates daily cleaning schedules with frequencies for wards, consultation rooms, theatres, toilets and public areas, assigns tasks to housekeeping staff, coordinates isolation-room cleaning with Infection Prevention and Control, tracks cleaning supplies and equipment, records healthcare waste collection and segregation, documents hygiene inspections with corrective actions and pest control, and manages cleanliness complaints to resolution.

Example. A patient is discharged: the room is flagged for cleaning, a task created and assigned, cleaning and disinfection recorded, inspection completed, and the room marked ready for its next patient.

Users. Housekeeping Manager, Cleaning Supervisors, Cleaning Personnel, Environmental Health Officers, IPC Officers, Waste Management Officers, Hospital Administrators.
