Start Discharge

Open a discharge process for a patient who is currently admitted. Starting the process never discharges anyone by itself — the responsible clinician must authorize it, the checklist must be completed, and the final confirmation on the details page ends the stay.

🚨 Emergency care must never wait for this form. If the patient needs urgent attention, treat them first.
