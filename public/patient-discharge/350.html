How the discharge half works

Starting a discharge never discharges anyone by itself. The responsible clinician authorizes it (Approved for Discharge), the checklist is completed step by step, and only then can the discharge be confirmed. Confirmation ends the active admission, releases the bed to Cleaning Required — never straight to Available — and keeps every historical record. A patient who leaves a bed does not automatically make the bed ready for the next patient.
