How Dental Department works

What it does. Registers dental patients against their Registration ID with complaints and relevant allergies, records oral examinations and dental charting (decayed, missing or damaged teeth), documents diagnoses, manages treatments (fillings, extractions, root canals, scaling), registers oral surgery with consent and anaesthesia, requests dental imaging, and schedules appointments.

Example. A patient arrives with severe tooth pain: the complaint is recorded, the oral examination and chart updated, the diagnosis documented, the procedure performed, and a follow-up scheduled if needed.

Users. Dentists, Dental Surgeons, Dental Therapists, Dental Nurses, Dental Assistants, Radiology Professionals, Dental Department Manager.
