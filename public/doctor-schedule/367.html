How Doctor Scheduling and Duty Roster works

What it does. Creates consultation schedules and duty rosters, assigns morning, afternoon and night shifts (including weekends and holidays), allocates doctors to departments, tracks availability and conflicts, records shift handovers with notes, and produces daily and weekly rosters.

Example. Three doctors cover Monday to Wednesday in turns of Morning / Afternoon / Night; the roster shows who is available in each department and shift, so staff can assign consultations to an available doctor.

Users. Hospital Administrator, Medical Director, Department Head, Scheduling Officer, Doctors, HR Officer. Staff management records who the doctors are; the roster records when and where they work.
