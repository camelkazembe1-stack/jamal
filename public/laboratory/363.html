Lab Workbench

One request at a time: collect or reject the specimen, run the tests, enter the results, and let an authorized person verify them. A result only counts as released once it is verified — and a critical result must be acknowledged by name.

Specimen

The specimen is tracked here: awaiting collection, collected, received, rejected (with a reason) or needing recollection. A rejected specimen blocks testing until a new one is collected.

Tests & results

Enter each result with its unit and reference range. Tick Abnormal when it falls outside the range, and Critical when a clinician must be told at once. Verification is a separate step — an unverified result is never treated as released.
