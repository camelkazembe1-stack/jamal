How the laboratory half works

A request is opened for an existing patient (never a second patient record). The specimen is collected — or rejected, with a reason — the tests run, and each result is entered with its unit and reference range. An authorized person verifies every result before it counts as released. Critical results must be acknowledged: who was notified, by whom, and when. Nothing here is hard-coded — every number on this page is calculated from the stored records.
