How Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation works

What it does. Registers rehabilitation referrals with reasons and history, records physical assessments (muscle strength, joint movement, mobility, pain levels), creates individual rehabilitation plans with targets and therapy frequency, schedules and records therapy sessions, tracks wheelchairs and walking aids, monitors progress against previous assessments, and documents completion with home exercise instructions.

Example. After leg-fracture surgery the patient is referred: assessment recorded, a plan created, sessions scheduled and logged, progress measured against the pain and mobility baseline, and rehabilitation completed with follow-up instructions.

Users. Physiotherapists, Rehabilitation Specialists, Occupational Therapists, Physiotherapy Assistants, Orthopaedic Specialists, Rehabilitation Department Manager.
