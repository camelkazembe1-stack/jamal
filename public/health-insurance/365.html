How Health Insurance works

What it does. Registers insurance companies and schemes, stores each patient's membership and policy details against their Registration ID, verifies active coverage and restrictions, creates and submits claims linked to invoices, and tracks each claim from Draft to Paid.

Example. Services worth TSh 40,000: the insurer covers TSh 35,000 and the patient contributes TSh 5,000. The claim for TSh 35,000 is submitted, tracked through Under Review, and closed when the insurer pays.

Users. Insurance Officer, Billing Officer, Cashier, Accountant, Hospital Administrator, Management. Billing totals the cost; insurance decides how much of it the insurer pays.
