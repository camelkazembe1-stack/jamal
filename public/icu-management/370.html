How Intensive Care Unit (ICU) works

What it does. Registers ICU admissions with reasons and referring doctors, tracks ICU beds (available, occupied, cleaning), keeps observation charts (heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, temperature), documents oxygen and ventilator support, medications and nursing care, and manages transfer to ordinary wards with a complete admission history.

Example. A patient with severe breathing complications: admission recorded, an ICU bed assigned, vital signs tracked at intervals, oxygen and ventilator support documented, and transfer to a general ward recorded when clinically authorised.

Users. ICU Doctors and Nurses, Critical Care Specialists, Respiratory Therapists, ICU Department Manager, Hospital Administrator.
