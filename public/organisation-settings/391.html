Organisation Settings

One organisation, one database: the profile printed on every business and health document, the modules this organisation uses, and a full backup of every record.

Organisation Profile

These details identify the organisation on invoices, hospital bills, receipts, statements and reports. Any country, any currency.

Modules this organisation uses

Switched-off modules leave the menu and Home; their records stay safe. Hospital bills, payments and paid insurance claims always post to the one set of books.

Backup & Restore

All records live in this browser's Local Storage. Clearing the browser, changing device or a broken computer loses them. Download a backup file regularly and keep it somewhere safe.

Restore replaces the records of every area included in the file. Records not in the file stay as they are.

Security — two-factor sign-in & device lock

Two protections for shared clinic computers, both running entirely in this browser with Web Crypto (no outside service): 2FA turns the authenticator app on your phone into a second key for this account, and the device lock is a password the browser asks for when the shell opens. The lock password follows the policy: at least 8 characters with at least 3 of lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols.
