How the imaging half works

A request is opened for an existing patient, with the requesting department and clinician recorded. Urgent requests jump ahead of routine ones in the queue. The appointment is scheduled (or rescheduled or missed, each with a reason), the procedure is performed, and the findings become a report that an authorized person must verify and finalize. Urgent findings must be acknowledged by name. Corrections are kept in the report's history — nothing is silently overwritten.
