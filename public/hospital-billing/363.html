How Hospital Billing and Invoicing works

What it does. Every service a patient receives is properly recorded, charged and included in the patient's bill. It maintains service pricing, generates invoices with a unique invoice number, tracks billing status (unpaid, partially paid, fully paid, overdue, cancelled) and records authorised discounts and exemptions.

Example. Consultation TSh 10,000 + laboratory TSh 15,000 + medication TSh 8,000 + treatment TSh 7,000 combine into one invoice of TSh 40,000 showing each service, the total, the amount already paid and the remaining balance.

Users. Cashier, Billing Officer, Hospital Administrator, Management. Billing answers: which services did the patient receive, how much does each cost, and how much is owed? Payments (a separate module) records the money actually received.
