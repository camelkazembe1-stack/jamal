How Payments and Financial works

What it does. Records full and partial payments, issues receipts, tracks payment methods (cash, mobile money, bank transfer, cards, insurance), manages deposits and advances, handles refund approvals and keeps the payment history behind every outstanding balance.

Example. An invoice of TSh 40,000 receives an initial payment of TSh 25,000 (receipt issued, balance TSh 15,000); when the remaining TSh 15,000 is paid, the invoice becomes Fully Paid.

Users. Cashier, Accountant, Finance Officer, Hospital Administrator, Management. Billing decides how much the patient owes; this module records the money actually received and spent.
