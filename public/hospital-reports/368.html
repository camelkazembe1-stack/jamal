How Hospital Reports and Analytics works

What it does. Patient reports (registrations, admissions, discharges, demographics), clinical reports (consultations, diagnoses, laboratory and imaging activity), departmental performance, financial summaries, staff activity, pharmacy and stock, and operational indicators such as bed occupancy and waiting times. Reports for a specific period can be filtered and printed.

Example. For November: 1,200 registered patients, 950 consultations, 600 laboratory tests, 150 admissions, 130 discharges, revenue TSh 15,000,000, outstanding TSh 2,000,000 — the numbers below are counted live from the stored records of every module.

Users. Hospital Administrator, Medical Director, Department Heads, Accountant, HR Officer, Management. Operational modules record the daily work; this page turns those records into indicators.
