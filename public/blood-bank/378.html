How Blood Bank and Transfusion works

What it does. Registers donors with eligibility history, records donations with unique unit numbers, maintains the blood-group inventory (ABO and Rh), tracks infectious-disease screening (only screened units may be released), monitors storage locations and expiry, manages crossmatching and compatibility results, reserves and allocates units to patients, and documents transfusions with observations and reactions.

Example. A patient needs transfusion after blood loss: the request is received, compatibility tested, suitable screened units reserved and authorised, issued to the department, and the transfusion documented with observations.

Users. Blood Bank Scientists, Laboratory Technologists, Blood Transfusion Officers, Haematologists, Medical Officers, Blood Bank Manager, Nursing Staff.
