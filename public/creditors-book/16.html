Money the business owes: credit purchases increase a supplier's balance; payments to suppliers and purchases returns reduce it. Balances are tracked per supplier, with the total outstanding payable at the end. Updates live.

Register a creditor brought forward

For a supplier the business already owed money before these books began - before the business or before JAMALI. Their balance enters the book as one Creditors Opening entry, dated today, and every later payment reduces it. It is not a purchase: the Purchases Journal never sees it.
