How Infection Prevention and Control works

What it does. Records suspected and confirmed infection cases linked to patients, monitors healthcare-associated infections (surgical site, catheter, ventilator), tracks trends and outbreak signals, manages isolation rooms with precautions and dates, records hand-hygiene and PPE compliance, documents outbreaks and staff exposure incidents with corrective actions, and produces surveillance reports.

Example. A patient is diagnosed with an infectious disease: the case is recorded against the Registration ID, precautions identified, an isolation room coordinated, authorised personnel notified, and isolation closed when authorised.

Users. IPC Officers, Infection Control Nurses, Medical Microbiologists, Hospital Epidemiologists, Ward Nurses, Environmental Health Officers, Hospital Management.
