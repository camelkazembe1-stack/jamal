How Mortuary works

What it does. Records deceased patients against their Registration ID with a unique mortuary reference number, documents death certification information and the responsible professional, assigns identification labels, manages storage locations and capacity, records next-of-kin notifications, tracks postmortem requests and authorisation, and manages body release with verified documentation and signed handover.

Example. A patient dies in hospital: the death is documented, identity recorded, a mortuary reference assigned, a storage location given, next of kin notified, and the body released only with verified authorisation to a confirmed recipient.

Users. Mortuary Attendants, Medical Officers, Pathologists, Hospital Administrators, Medical Records Officers, Authorised Release Officers.
