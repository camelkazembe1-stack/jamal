How Operating Theatre and Surgery works

What it does. Registers planned operations with patient details and the reason for surgery, schedules operation dates and theatres without conflicts, assigns surgeons, anaesthetists and theatre nurses, records preoperative assessment and consent, documents anaesthesia and intraoperative findings, and tracks postoperative recovery and transfer.

Example. A patient requires an operation: surgery registered, theatre and team assigned, consent and anaesthesia assessments documented, operation performed and recorded, postoperative care noted, and the patient transferred to recovery, ICU or a ward.

Users. Surgeons, Anaesthetists, Theatre Nurses, Surgical Assistants, Theatre Manager, Recovery Room Nurses, Hospital Administrator. Treatment & Procedures covers routine care; this module covers formal theatre surgery.
