How Immunisation and Vaccination works

What it does. Registers vaccination patients against their Registration ID, maintains the recommended schedules with doses and intervals, records each administration (vaccine, dose number, site, route, professional), tracks vaccine stock, batches and expiry with cold-chain temperatures, records adverse events following immunisation, and follows up missed doses with reminders.

Example. A six-week-old baby attends: previous vaccinations checked, vaccines due identified, stock and batch confirmed, doses administered and recorded, and the next date set.

Users. Immunisation Nurses, Vaccination Officers, Paediatric Nurses, Public Health Officers, Pharmacy Staff, Cold Chain Officers, Immunisation Programme Manager.
