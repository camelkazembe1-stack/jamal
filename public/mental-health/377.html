How Mental Health Services works

What it does. Registers patients for mental health services against their Registration ID with confidentiality and restricted access, records psychological assessments and behavioural observations, documents psychiatric consultations and diagnoses, assesses self-harm and harm-to-others risk with safety plans, manages treatment plans and counselling sessions, monitors psychiatric medication and side effects, and handles specialised admissions and follow-up.

Example. A patient with persistent distress and sleeping difficulty: registered, concerns documented, assessment conducted, treatment plan created, counselling scheduled, progress monitored at follow-up.

Users. Psychiatrists, Clinical Psychologists, Counselling Psychologists, Mental Health Nurses, Psychiatric Social Workers, Mental Health Department Manager.
