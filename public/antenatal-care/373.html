How Antenatal and Postnatal Care works

What it does. Registers pregnant women against their Registration ID with pregnancy history and estimated delivery dates, records assessments (weight, blood pressure, temperature), tracks blood group, haemoglobin and screenings, manages pregnancy risks (hypertension, gestational diabetes), schedules antenatal visits, documents preventive care and birth preparedness, and follows the mother after childbirth.

Example. A first antenatal visit: record opened, pregnancy history recorded, EDD calculated, measurements taken, laboratory investigations requested, risk assessed, and the next appointment scheduled.

Users. Obstetricians, Midwives, Antenatal and Postnatal Nurses, Laboratory Professionals, Maternal Health Specialists, Maternity Department Manager.
