How Chronic Disease works

What it does. Registers patients with chronic conditions against their Registration ID, records clinical monitoring (blood pressure, glucose, weight, laboratory results), maintains individual treatment plans and long-term medication records with refills and side effects, schedules routine reviews, tracks complications and warning signs, and documents patient education and lifestyle advice.

Example. A patient diagnosed with diabetes: enrolled, diagnosis and baseline glucose recorded, a treatment plan created, medication and follow-up scheduled, and control monitored over time.

Users. Physicians, Medical Officers, Diabetes Specialists, Nurses, Clinical Officers, Nutritionists, Chronic Disease Programme Coordinator.
