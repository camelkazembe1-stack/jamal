How Medical Equipment works

What it does. Registers equipment with unique identification numbers, manufacturers, serial numbers and warranty dates, allocates devices to departments and wards, tracks availability (in use, faulty, under maintenance), schedules preventive maintenance and calibration with expiry dates, records repairs with technicians and costs, and documents disposal of obsolete equipment with the full service history.

Example. A new patient monitor is purchased: registered with serial and warranty, assigned to the ICU, preventive maintenance scheduled, calibration tracked, and repairs recorded when faults occur.

Users. Biomedical Engineers and Technicians, Equipment Maintenance Officers, Department Heads, Procurement Officers, Hospital Administrators. Equipment tracks individual devices; General Inventory tracks quantities of stock.
