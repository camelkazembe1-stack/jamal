How Staff and Human Resource works

What it does. Registers employees with unique staff identification numbers, records qualifications, licences and their expiry dates, assigns departments and positions, tracks employment type (permanent, temporary, contract), attendance, leave requests and approvals, and stores employment documents.

Example. A new doctor is registered, given a staff number, their licence and its expiry recorded, assigned to the outpatient department, and their attendance and leave history maintained.

Users. HR Officer, Department Head, Hospital Administrator, Medical Director, Accountant, Management. Staff management records who the employees are; Doctor Scheduling records when they work.
