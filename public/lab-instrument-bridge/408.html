Lab Instrument Bridge

The doorway between the bench machines and the records: bring a results file from an analyser (HL7 ORU, ASTM-style, or a plain CSV the machine exports), check each line against the open lab requests by Registration ID, and accept the good ones straight into the workbench. Completed results can be exported back as HL7 ORU for a connected system.
