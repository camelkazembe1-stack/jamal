FHIR Bridge

The records speak FHIR R4, the international health-data language: build a real FHIR Bundle (Patient, Encounter, Condition with ICD-10, Observation with LOINC, MedicationRequest) out of this device's records, push it to any FHIR endpoint, or validate a bundle someone sent. Every resource carries the patient's single Registration ID as its identifier.
