Where alerts are delivered

Dashboard and the in-app bell are delivered inside Jamal. SMS and email are queued as ready-to-send texts in the Notification Center — one tap hands them to the phone's own messaging or mail app, so no external gateway is needed. Browser push pops a system notification while Jamal is open.
