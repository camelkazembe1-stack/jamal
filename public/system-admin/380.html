Data Deletion Requests

Organisation users cannot delete shared data directly — from Settings → Show Delete Data they send a deletion request with a required reason, and it lands here. Nothing is ever deleted before your approval. Approve shows a final confirmation with exactly which data will be deleted, executes the permanent deletion immediately (a retention copy is archived into the deletion audit first), records you as the deciding administrator with the date and time, and notifies the user. Reject requires a reason, keeps every document untouched, and notifies the user. Pending, approved, rejected, completed and failed requests are all kept here.
