How Emergency Department works

What it does. Registers emergency arrivals (including unidentified patients), performs emergency triage with vital signs and urgency classes, manages resuscitation, records urgent treatments and interventions, tracks the emergency workflow (waiting → treatment → disposition) and closes every case with a disposition: home, ward admission, ICU, another hospital, specialist referral, or death.

Example. A road-accident patient arrives: the arrival is registered, triage classifies the case Critical, the emergency team is alerted, immediate treatment is recorded, and the final disposition (ICU or ward) is documented.

Users. Emergency Doctors and Nurses, Triage Nurses, Resuscitation Team, Emergency Department Manager, Hospital Administrator.
