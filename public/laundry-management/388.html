How Laundry works

What it does. Registers linen items (sheets, pillowcases, blankets, gowns, uniforms), records dirty linen collected from wards with quantities and handling separation, tracks washing, disinfection, drying and ironing, manages clean-linen storage separated from contaminated, distributes linen to departments with returns, and records damaged, missing and discarded items with replacement history.

Example. Maternity sends 100 dirty sheets: collected and recorded against the department, washed and disinfected, dried and ironed, the clean quantity updated, and the sheets distributed back to maternity.

Users. Laundry Officers and Supervisors, Laundry Workers, Ward Managers, Housekeeping Officers, Hospital Administrators.
