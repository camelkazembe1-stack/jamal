Ward Management

Wards, beds, occupancy, assignments, transfers, cleaning and the registers — the placement half of the medical wing. Occupancy is always counted from the bed records, never typed in. Emergency treatment always comes first; no form here holds up care.

How the ward half works

Register the ward first (331), then put its beds on the map (332). The occupancy dashboard (333) counts every bed by its status — a bed is assignable only while it is marked Available. Bed Assignment (334) connects an existing patient to a free bed — it never creates a second patient record. Transfers (335) free the old bed and fill the new one in one atomic move, and the history is preserved. Cleaning & Maintenance (336) keeps unusable beds out of the assignment lists until they are ready. The registers (337, 338) remember every placement and every move, including the completed ones.

Patient Admission (page 340) reuses this half: an admission links the patient’s stay to the ward and bed records kept here — the two halves never duplicate each other.
