Patient-to-Bed Assignment

Connect an existing patient to an available bed. Only beds marked Available are listed, a patient can hold only one active placement, and every assignment is remembered. Bed placement is not the same as admission — the admission record (page 341) manages the stay; this page records where the patient lies.

🚨 Emergency rule: urgent care is never delayed by a bed assignment. Treat first — when no suitable bed is free, the dashboard (333) shows the true situation and the emergency procedure of the facility applies. An occupied bed is never shown as available.
