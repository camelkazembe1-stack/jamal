How General Hospital Inventory works

What it does. Registers stock items with codes, categories and minimum levels, records incoming stock linked to suppliers and purchase orders, organises storage locations, issues items to departments with receiving personnel, transfers stock between stores, monitors low and out-of-stock items with replenishment alerts, tracks expiry, damage and adjustments, and supports physical stocktaking with discrepancy records.

Example. 500 pairs of gloves are received and placed in the main store; 100 pairs are issued to outpatients; the balance updates to 400 and the storekeeper is alerted when the minimum level is reached.

Users. Storekeepers, Inventory Officers, Pharmacy Store Officers, Department Heads, Procurement Officers, Warehouse Managers, Hospital Administrators.
