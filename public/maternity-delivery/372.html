How Maternity and Delivery works

What it does. Registers maternity admissions with pregnancy details and estimated delivery dates, tracks labour progress (contractions, cervical assessments, maternal vitals), records the delivery method (normal, assisted or caesarean), creates the newborn record with APGAR scores and birth weight, monitors mother and newborn, and manages discharge.

Example. A patient arrives in labour: identified by Registration ID, labour documented and monitored, delivery recorded with the newborn's APGAR and weight, postpartum care tracked, and mother and baby discharged when authorised.

Users. Obstetricians, Midwives, Maternity Nurses, Gynaecologists, Neonatal Nurses, Paediatricians, Maternity Department Manager. Antenatal Care manages check-ups before and after delivery; this module manages the delivery itself.
